Which service is right for me?

Choose Strategy Session if:

You have 1-2 specific questions (like "How do I better organize my finances?" or "How should I start investing?") and want targeted guidance without a full plan.

Choose Full Financial Plan if:

You want a comprehensive view of your entire financial picture with a written roadmap covering retirement, investing, cash flow, and other major planning areas.

Choose Ongoing Planning if:

Your financial situation is complex or changing, and you want continued support and accountability as you work toward your goals over time.

FAQ

Which service should I choose?

Choose the Strategy Session if you have 1-2 specific questions. Choose the Full Financial Plan if you want a comprehensive written roadmap. Choose Ongoing Planning if you want continued support as your situation evolves.

How do I pay for the Strategy Session?

Payment is collected at the time of booking through the scheduling link. The fee is non-refundable after the session.

Can I reschedule my Strategy Session?

You may reschedule up to two times at no additional cost, as long as changes are made at least 48 hours before your session. A third reschedule requires a new fee. Missed sessions without the required notice will result in a forfeited fee.

Can I upgrade from a Strategy Session to a Full Plan?

Yes. If you decide you'd like more comprehensive planning after a Strategy Session, we can discuss moving forward with a Full Financial Plan. The $225 Strategy Session fee is separate and does not apply toward the Full Plan fee.

What's included in the Full Financial Plan?

The Full Financial Plan includes an in-depth intake process, review of relevant documents, analysis across multiple planning areas (retirement, investing, cash flow, risk management), a written financial plan with prioritized recommendations, and multiple planning meetings over a defined engagement period.

How does payment work for the Full Financial Plan and Ongoing Planning?

The Full Financial Plan fee ($2,500) is paid in advance before services begin. Ongoing Planning is billed quarterly in advance at $875 per quarter. If you terminate either service early, any unearned fees will be refunded.

What's the annual plan update for Ongoing Planning clients?

Clients in the Ongoing Planning Relationship who have previously completed a paid Full Financial Plan may receive an annual update to their original plan once cumulative ongoing fees paid equal or exceed the original Full Plan fee. This update is limited in scope and reflects changes to your circumstances.

Do you manage investments?

No. All services are advice-only. You keep full control and implement your own decisions with your chosen service provider.

Do you take custody of accounts or place trades?

No. The firm does not take custody, execute trades, or monitor accounts on an ongoing basis.

What don't you do?

I do not manage investments on an ongoing basis, charge asset-based fees (AUM billing), place trades or monitor accounts continuously, sell products or receive commissions, have affiliations with financial firms or product providers, or take control of your money. You maintain full control and are responsible for making any changes with your chosen provider.
